About Me

Hey, I'm Priyadharshini — but everyone knows me as Aliyaa! I'm an Electronics and Communication Engineering student at RIT Chennai with a growing passion for building things at the intersection of technology and design. While my core studies are rooted in electronics, I've found myself increasingly drawn to the world of web development, UI/UX design, and creative technology — spaces where I get to turn ideas into interactive, meaningful experiences.

I'm currently sharpening my development skills by building real projects from scratch, working with tools like React, Tailwind CSS, and Supabase. Some of my favorite builds so far include Folded, a calming digital keepsake app for collecting memories, and Raksha, a health-safety companion concept designed with real-world impact in mind. I love the process of discovery-oriented learning — figuring things out hands-on rather than just reading about them.

Outside of coding, I'm active in my college's entrepreneurship cell, where I get to collaborate on hackathons and innovation-driven projects. I also enjoy public speaking and event hosting — there's something exciting about connecting with people, whether that's through a stage mic or a line of code. I'm always looking for new challenges that let me learn, build, and create things that genuinely matter.
